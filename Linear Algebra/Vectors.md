# Vectors

## Overview

A **vector** is an object that can be added to other vectors and multiplied by scalars.

In linear algebra, vectors are often represented as columns of numbers:

$$
v =
\begin{bmatrix}
v_1 \\
v_2 \\
\vdots \\
v_n
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Vectors can represent directions, points, states, signals, or lists of coordinates.

## In Quantum Mechanics

In quantum mechanics, state vectors usually live in complex vector spaces.

They are often written using [Bra-Ket Notation](Bra-Ket%20Notation.md):

$$
|\psi\rangle
$$
