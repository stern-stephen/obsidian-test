# Phase Space

## Overview

Phase space is the space of generalized coordinates and conjugate momenta.

## Definition

For $n$ degrees of freedom, a phase-space point has coordinates:

$$
(q_1,\ldots,q_n,p_1,\ldots,p_n)
$$

## Intuition

A point in configuration space tells where the system is. A point in phase space also tells how it is moving, through the momenta.

## Why It Matters

Hamiltonian mechanics treats motion as a flow through phase space. Poisson brackets and canonical transformations are naturally defined there.
