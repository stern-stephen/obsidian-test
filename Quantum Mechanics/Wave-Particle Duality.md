# Wave-Particle Duality

## Overview

Wave-particle duality is the fact that microscopic systems can produce localized detections while their probability distributions display wave interference.

It is not best understood as a particle sometimes literally becoming a classical wave. The quantum state carries probability amplitudes, and amplitudes can interfere.

## Double-Slit Lesson

With both slits open, amplitudes add:

$$
\psi=\psi_1+\psi_2
$$

Probabilities come from squared magnitudes:

$$
P=|\psi|^2
$$

So the total pattern contains interference terms that would be absent if each detection were governed by an ordinary classical either-or path.

## Matter Waves

Matter can show interference too. De Broglie's relation links momentum and wavelength:

$$
p=\frac{h}{\lambda}
$$

or:

$$
p=\hbar k
$$
