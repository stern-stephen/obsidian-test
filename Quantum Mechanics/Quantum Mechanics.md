# Quantum Mechanics

## Overview

Quantum mechanics describes physical systems using states, measurements, and transformations on complex vector spaces.

For the Nielsen and Chuang reading project, this page is the hub for quantum mechanics concepts that recur across the book.

## Core Ideas

- Quantum states are represented using [[Linear Algebra/Bra-Ket Notation|bra-ket notation]].
- State spaces are complex [[Linear Algebra/Vector Spaces|vector spaces]].
- Reversible evolution is represented by [[Linear Algebra/Unitary Matrices|unitary operators]].
- Measurable quantities are represented by [[Linear Algebra/Hermitian Matrices and Operators|Hermitian operators]].
