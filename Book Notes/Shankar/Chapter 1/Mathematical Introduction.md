## Big Ideas

- Chapter 1 builds the vector-space language needed for quantum mechanics.
- The core move is to stop identifying vectors with arrows or columns only. A vector is an abstract object; arrows, columns, and functions are different representations of vectors.
- Linear operators become matrices after choosing a basis.
- Eigenvectors give the basis in which an operator acts most simply.
- Sections 1.9 and 1.10 bridge finite-dimensional matrix ideas to functions, wavefunctions, and continuous bases.
