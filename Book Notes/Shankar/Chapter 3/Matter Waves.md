## Notes

The double-slit logic extends from light to matter. Electrons, which are normally introduced as particles with mass and charge, can produce interference patterns.

This motivates de Broglie's association of a wavelength with matter. Momentum and wavelength are linked by:

$$
p=\frac{h}{\lambda}
$$

or equivalently:

$$
p=\hbar k
$$

The important point is not that an electron is secretly just a classical wave. It is that matter requires a state description capable of producing wave interference and particle-like detections.
