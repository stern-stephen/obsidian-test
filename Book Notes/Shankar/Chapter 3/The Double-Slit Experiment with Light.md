## Notes

Light displays interference, so in the wave regime it looks natural to treat it as a classical wave. The quantum tension appears when the light intensity is reduced until detections occur one at a time.

Each detection is localized, as if a particle arrived at the screen. But after many detections, the accumulated pattern is the wave interference pattern. The result is not well described by saying that each photon went through exactly one slit in an ordinary classical sense.

## Main Lesson

The wave-like object is not a directly observed smeared-out photon. It is a probability amplitude whose squared magnitude predicts the distribution of many localized detections.
