## Notes

Shankar starts by making the classical distinction sharp. A classical particle is localized, carries its energy and momentum as a small object, and follows a definite trajectory. A classical wave is spread out, can pass through multiple openings at once, and displays interference.

This setup matters because quantum mechanics will not merely add new forces to classical mechanics. It changes the categories used to describe microscopic systems.

## Key Contrast

| Classical entity | Typical behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Particle | Localized detection, definite path, additive intensities |
| Wave | Extended disturbance, superposition, interference |
