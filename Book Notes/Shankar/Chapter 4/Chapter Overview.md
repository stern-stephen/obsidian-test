## Big Ideas

- Chapter 4 states the postulates of nonrelativistic quantum mechanics for one particle in one dimension.
- The state is a Hilbert-space vector, not a point in classical phase space.
- Observables are Hermitian operators, and measurement outcomes are eigenvalues.
- Measurement is probabilistic and generally changes the state.
- Time evolution is governed by the Schrodinger equation with a Hamiltonian operator.
- The chapter translates the physical motivation from the double-slit experiment into a working mathematical rulebook.
