## Big Ideas

- Chapter 2 reviews the classical mechanics that quantum mechanics will later modify.
- The central language is not only Newton's force law, but also action, Lagrangians, Hamiltonians, phase space, Poisson brackets, and symmetries.
- Lagrangian mechanics emphasizes paths in configuration space.
- Hamiltonian mechanics emphasizes motion in phase space using coordinates and conjugate momenta.
- Symmetries generate conserved quantities, foreshadowing the operator and commutator language of quantum mechanics.
