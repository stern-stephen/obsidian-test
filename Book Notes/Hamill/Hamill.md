# Hamill

## Overview

This is the reading hub for Patrick Hamill's *A Student's Guide to Lagrangians and Hamiltonians*.

PDF reference: [Hamill](../../References/Hamill.pdf)

Hamill presents analytical mechanics as a student-oriented path from fundamental mechanical concepts through Lagrangian mechanics, Hamiltonian mechanics, canonical transformations, Hamilton-Jacobi theory, and continuous systems.
