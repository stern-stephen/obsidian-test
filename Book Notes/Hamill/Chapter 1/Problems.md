## Problem Set Focus

The Chapter 1 problems practice the basic mechanics vocabulary needed before the formal variational derivations:

- choosing generalized coordinates;
- identifying constraints and degrees of freedom;
- computing kinetic and potential energies;
- constructing Lagrangians;
- recognizing cyclic coordinates;
- identifying conserved generalized momenta;
- connecting symmetries with conservation laws.

## Notes For Future Exercise Answers

- Keep problem statements short and refer to the problem number only.
- For coordinate-choice problems, state the degrees of freedom before writing equations.
- For Lagrangian construction, write $T$, $V$, and $L=T-V$ separately.
- For cyclic-coordinate problems, compute the conjugate momentum explicitly.
- For conservation-law questions, say which symmetry is responsible.
