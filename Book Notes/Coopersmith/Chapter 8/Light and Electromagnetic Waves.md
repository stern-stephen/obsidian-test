## Least Time In Optics

The principle of least time is an optical version of stationary action. Comparing nearby light paths produces the laws of reflection and refraction. Coopersmith connects this extremal behavior to light's role as the limiting speed for signal transmission.

## Coupled Electric And Magnetic Fields

An electromagnetic wave consists of changing electric and magnetic fields that continually generate one another according to Maxwell's equations. Their rates of change must remain coordinated: unchecked reinforcement or cancellation would not describe a stable propagating wave.

Coopersmith interprets their shared propagation speed and coordinated oscillation as an action statement. The electromagnetic action packages the mutual evolution of the fields into one variational condition.
