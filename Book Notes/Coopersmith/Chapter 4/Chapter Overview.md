## Big Ideas

- Coopersmith presents the principle of virtual work as the statics version of the variational viewpoint.
- Newtonian statics balances forces particle by particle, while virtual work tests one summed scalar condition.
- Reaction and constraint forces disappear only when the virtual displacements are compatible with the constraints.
- Generalized coordinates extend the method beyond point particles, Cartesian displacements, and ordinary forces.
- The principle is powerful precisely because it can ignore hidden internal mechanisms when their virtual work vanishes.

## Logical Progression

1. Start with static equilibrium for non-interacting particles.
2. Recast equilibrium as a scalar virtual-work condition.
3. Show that rigid-body translations and rotations reproduce the usual zero resultant force and zero resultant moment conditions.
4. Separate net forces into applied and constraint parts.
5. Restrict virtual displacements so constraint forces do no virtual work.
6. Generalize from Cartesian particle displacements to generalized coordinates and generalized forces.
7. Use examples to show why hidden internal mechanisms need not be solved explicitly.
8. Present Lanczos's reaction-force version of the principle.
